Our Story

Chef Ravi — Bringing Authentic Indian Cuisine to Your Table

Marigold was founded on a belief: that Indian food should feel both genuine and welcoming. Not a watered-down approximation, but the real thing — dishes that honor age-old recipes while inviting new tastes to the table.

Chef-Entrepreneur Ravi grew up immersed in the aromas, spices, and stories of Indian kitchens. After years of training, traveling across India, and refining his craft, he brought that heritage to Eastchester. At Marigold, you'll taste recipes rooted in classic regional flavors — North, South, East, and West — each dish carefully composed to balance spice, texture, and richness.

The kitchen works with fresh, high-quality ingredients and traditional techniques: tandoori, slow braises, hand-rolled breads. Whether you're vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free, there is a place at this table for you.
