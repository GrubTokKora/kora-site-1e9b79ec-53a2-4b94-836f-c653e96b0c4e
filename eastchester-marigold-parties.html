Common Inquiries

Planning Your Private Event

Here are the key details organizers ask when planning private dinners and group gatherings at Marigold.

What group sizes can Marigold accommodate?

We accommodate groups from small parties of 6 to 20 guests in semi-private dining sections, up to full restaurant buyouts for larger private celebrations and corporate functions.

What dining styles are available for private parties?

We offer flexible formats to match your event style: multi-course plated dining, family-style sharing platters placed at each table, or hot buffet setups. Custom menu tastings can also be arranged on request.

Can you accommodate guests with vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free diets?

Yes. Authentic regional Indian cuisine is naturally well-suited to diverse diets. Chef Ravi prepares dedicated vegetarian specialties, dairy-free vegan dishes, and gluten-free curries and biryanis.

Is beverage service included?

Marigold provides full bar service including handcrafted cocktails, fine wines, and Indian and domestic beers. We can structure beverage packages or host bar tabs based on your event preferences.

How do we confirm availability and lock in our date?

Submit our enquiry form below or call us directly at (914) 202-9455 with your target date, approximate guest count, and occasion. We will respond promptly with package proposals and room availability.
