Common Inquiries

Questions About Our Specials & Happy Hour

Everything you need to know before visiting Marigold for our weekly promotions and dining discounts in Eastchester.

When does Marigold host Happy Hour in Eastchester?

Our Happy Hour runs Monday through Thursday from 4:30 PM to 6:30 PM. During these hours, selected appetizers are priced at $7.00, and all cocktails, beers, and wines by the glass are half-price.

Which appetizers are included in the $7 Happy Hour deal?

The $7 menu includes Baigan Burrata (smoked eggplant with burrata), Amritsar Fish, Chilli Chicken, Apricot Chicken Sheek Kebab, Paprichat, and Bhej Puri. Each item is freshly cooked in our kitchen using authentic regional spices.

Do I need a reservation to take advantage of Happy Hour?

Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed at our bar and in the dining room during Happy Hour. If you are visiting with a larger group or wish to guarantee a specific dining table, we recommend making a reservation online or calling us at (914) 202-9455.

How does the weekend Early Bird special work?

Every Saturday and Sunday from 11:30 AM to 3:00 PM, guests can order our 3-Course Special complete with a champagne toast for $40 per person. In addition, guests who dine before 2:00 PM on weekends receive 15% off their entire bill when spending $120 or more.

Are dietary restrictions accommodating during specials?

Yes! We offer rich vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-friendly options throughout our Happy Hour and daily menus. Please inform your server of any dietary needs or allergies so Chef Ravi's team can prepare your meal safely.
