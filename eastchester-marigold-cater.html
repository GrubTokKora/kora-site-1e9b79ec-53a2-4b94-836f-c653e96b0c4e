The Marigold Standard

Why Choose Marigold Catering

We bring the same culinary discipline that earned recognition in Forbes and Tri-State dining guides directly to your catered table.

Michelin-Trained Chef Leadership

Every catering menu is designed and overseen by Chef-Entrepreneur Ravi. Rather than generic buffet sauces, our gravies, marinades, and braises are prepared from scratch with authentic regional recipes.

Fresh, High-Quality Whole Spices

We use freshly roasted and ground spices, uncompromised meats, fresh produce, and real dairy. Our butter chicken, biryanis, and tandoori meats taste vibrant, aromatic, and distinctly home-crafted.

Inclusive Dietary Accommodations

Hosting guests with diverse dietary needs is effortless. Our extensive menu offers certified halal meats, rich vegetarian preparations, dairy-free vegan dishes, and naturally gluten-free curries clearly marked.

Punctual Timing & Polished Presentation

Timing is paramount in catering. We arrive promptly, deliver food piping hot, and ensure every platter, chafer, and garnish is arranged cleanly and gracefully before your first guest steps through the door.
