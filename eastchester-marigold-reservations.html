Good to know

Dining Room Guidelines

Table Holding Policy

We hold reserved tables for 15 minutes past your booked time. If your party is running late along White Plains Road, please call us at (914) 202-9455 so we can keep your table ready.

Dietary & Allergen Care

Whether you require vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or specific spice levels, our kitchen accommodates your needs. Please let your server know your preferences when seated.

Walk-In Guests Welcome

While reservations are strongly recommended for weekend dinners and Sunday dining, we always reserve space for walk-in neighbors and bar seating on a first-come basis.
