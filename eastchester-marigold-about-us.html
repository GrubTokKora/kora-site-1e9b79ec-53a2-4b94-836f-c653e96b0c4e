The Inspiration & Heritage

Tradition with Innovation in Eastchester

Marigold was founded on a simple, uncompromising belief: that Indian food in America should feel both profoundly genuine and warmly welcoming. Rather than offering dishes ladle-poured from an all-purpose sauce base, Chef-Entrepreneur Ravi and his culinary partners set out to celebrate the diverse culinary cultures of India right here on White Plains Road in Eastchester.

Chef Ravi grew up immersed in the aromas, spices, and generational narratives of authentic Indian kitchens. After extensive training under master chefs, traveling across the subcontinent, and honing classical techniques, he developed a personal philosophy: honor timeless recipes while inviting contemporary nuance to the plate.

“A great chef turns simple ingredients into unforgettable experiences. Cook with heart, and the world will taste the difference. Here is proof. Meet Ravinder Bisht of 434 White Plains Rd, Eastchester.”

— Mina Oza, Guest & Eastchester Resident

At Marigold, each preparation reflects its geographical origins. The spice profiles do not simply deliver heat; they build harmony, aroma, and depth. From the rich Kashmiri spices in our goat rogan josh to the roasted coconut and peppercorns of Chettinad chicken, every plate honors the region it was born in.

The kitchen relies on rigorous classical methods: high-heat clay tandoor roasting, slow aromatic braises, and hand-rolled breads baked to order on the oven wall. Whether you join us for an anniversary dinner, a casual weekday Happy Hour, or a multi-generational family milestone, Chef Ravi and our service team are dedicated to making every guest feel valued.

Our Mission: To bring the authentic flavors of India to your table with honesty, care, and creativity. We aim to be more than just an Eastchester restaurant — Marigold is a warm neighborhood home where memories are made and the love for culinary heritage is shared.

The Subcontinent on Your Plate

A Tour of Regional Flavors

Chef Ravi refuses the generic "one curry sauce" approach. Here is how our kitchen brings distinct regions of India to Eastchester:

North

Tandoor & Rich Mughlai

Slow-simmered Delhi-style Butter Chicken, spiced Lamb Chops, clay-fired Naan, and aromatic Dal Makhani cooked over gentle heat.

South

Coconut & Black Pepper

Chettinad Chicken infused with fresh coconut milk and roasted spices, fragrant tomato and coconut rice, and delicate seafood.

East

Mustard, Fish & Chaat

Crispy Amritsari fish, Baigan Bhaja with tamarind glaze, street-corner papri and samosa chaats balanced with cooling mint yogurt.

West

Goan Spice & Tang

Traditional Goan-style Chicken & Lamb Vindaloo simmered with whole red chilies, vinegar, and roasted coconut masala curries.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dining with Chef Ravi at Marigold in Eastchester.

Who is Chef Ravi and what is his background?
Chef-Entrepreneur Ravi (Ravinder Bisht) trained with master chefs in India and has extensive culinary experience across prestigious dining rooms, including Michelin-caliber kitchens. At Marigold, he oversees a kitchen dedicated to regional Indian authenticity, balancing classic family recipes with modern culinary technique.
What makes Marigold's food different from other Indian restaurants?
As highlighted by Forbes, Marigold avoids the standard shortcut of using a single base sauce for multiple dishes. Every recipe is created from scratch with distinct regional spice profiles spanning North, South, East, and West India, utilizing traditional clay tandoor roasting, slow braising, and fresh-baked breads.
Does Chef Ravi accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free diets?
Yes, inclusive dining is central to our philosophy. Our menu features a large selection of dedicated vegetarian entrees (such as Baigan Burrata, Paneer Kundan Kalia, and Methi Malai Matar), plant-based vegan preparations, and naturally gluten-free curries and tandoori grills. Please inform your server of any dietary needs or food allergies when ordering.
Do you offer full bar service and wine pairings?
Yes. Marigold features a full bar offering handcrafted specialty cocktails, selected draft and bottled beers, and thoughtfully curated wine pairings designed to complement the bold, layered spices of regional Indian cooking.
Can Chef Ravi cater private events or host large celebrations?
Yes. Marigold hosts private parties, birthdays, anniversaries, and corporate events with semi-private sections or full restaurant buyouts (learn about private parties). We also provide full-service and drop-off catering across Westchester (explore catering options).
Where is Marigold located and what are your operating hours?
Marigold is located at 434 White Plains Road, Eastchester, NY 10709. We are open Monday through Thursday for lunch (11:30 am–3:00 pm) and dinner (4:30 pm–10:00 pm); Friday & Saturday continuous from 11:30 am to 10:00 pm; and Sunday continuous from 11:30 am to 9:30 pm.

Information updated June 2025. Chef Ravi welcomes you to experience genuine Indian warmth.
