Everything You Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions About Dining at Marigold

Have questions about our kitchen, ingredients, or dining options? Here are the details guests ask most often.

Do you accommodate vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free dietary needs?

Yes. Marigold features an extensive dedicated vegetarian menu including paneer specialties, summer squash dumplings, lentils, and eggplant dishes. Many curries and tandoor grilled proteins are naturally gluten-free. Please let your server know of any dietary requirements or food allergies before ordering.

What is included with the Lunch Specials?

Our lunch specials are available during lunch hours (11:30 am–3:00 pm) and include your choice of one entrée served complete with fragrant basmati rice, slow-cooked dal, and fresh naan baked in our tandoor oven, starting at $15.00.

When is Happy Hour held at Marigold?

Happy Hour runs Monday through Thursday from 4:30 PM to 6:30 PM. During this time, select signature appetizers are $7.00 each and cocktails, wines, and beers are half price.

How does the cash and credit card pricing work?

In accordance with New York regulations, our establishment applies dual pricing for credit card and cash transactions as clearly displayed on itemized receipts.

Are reservations recommended for dinner in Eastchester?

Yes. To guarantee seating for your party, particularly on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday evenings, we strongly recommend reserving your table online or calling our team at (914) 202-9455.

Do you offer large group dining and off-site catering?

Yes. We host private parties, birthdays, and corporate gatherings in our dining room, and we provide both drop-off and full-service catering across Eastchester and Westchester County. Visit our Private Parties or Catering pages to enquire.

Ready to taste Chef Ravi's dishes?

Join us for lunch or dinner at 434 White Plains Road, Eastchester. Reservations recommended.
